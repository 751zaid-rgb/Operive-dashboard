Services

Productized services that ship a working workflow.

01

AI Workflow Audit

Find the one workflow worth fixing first.

We review where time, lead quality, and follow-up are breaking down, then prioritize one workflow with a clear implementation path.

  • Tool inventory
  • Workflow recommendation

Implementation Sprints

Install one practical workflow with bounded scope.

Short, focused delivery sprints to configure prompts, automations, routing, and team usage patterns around a real operational outcome.

  • Workflow design
  • Tool configuration
  • QA and handoff
  • Documentation

03

Ongoing AI Ops Support

Keep the workflow working after launch.

We monitor what is working, refine prompts and rules, and fix friction before it turns into abandoned tooling.

  • Monthly review
  • Adjustment backlog
  • Usage refinement
  • Performance notes

04

Embedded Team Training

Get adoption during implementation, not after it stalls.

Training is built into rollout, so your team knows when to use AI, when to review manually, and what good output looks like.

  • Manager handoff

Trust & Safety Support

Use AI responsibly, with clear guardrails.

We help you define practical rules for data handling, human review, tool access, and acceptable use inside daily workflows.
