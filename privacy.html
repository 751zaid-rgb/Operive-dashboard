Information submitted through the contact form or email may be used to review your request, respond to you, assess fit, and maintain normal business records.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Operive uses reasonable efforts appropriate for a small-business website and business operations, but cannot promise that data transmission or storage will always be completely secure.
