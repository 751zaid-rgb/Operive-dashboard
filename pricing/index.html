Pricing FAQ

The questions that decide a budget.

Is there a setup fee?

Yes. Operive is implementation-first. The setup fee covers workflow design, configuration, testing, and rollout.

Can I cancel monthly?

Yes, standard plans are month-to-month unless you choose annual billing.

Do you offer annual discounts?

Yes. Annual billing saves 10%.

Are SMS, WhatsApp, phone, and voice costs included?

Each plan includes message credits. Usage beyond included credits or special carrier/channel fees may be billed separately.

Do I need to understand AI tokens?

No. Operive prices around workflow scope and usage, not raw AI tokens.

Which plan should I start with?

Most businesses should start with the AI Workflow Audit, or the Growth plan if you already know you are losing leads from missed calls, slow replies, or manual follow-up.

Is Operive suitable for healthcare-related businesses?

Operive can support health-adjacent workflows, but regulated data, privacy, and compliance requirements must be scoped carefully. Compliance-specific workflows may require custom pricing and additional safeguards.

Can you connect to my CRM or calendar?

Usually yes, depending on the system. Integrations are confirmed during the audit or setup process.
