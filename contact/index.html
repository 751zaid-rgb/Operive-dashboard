FAQ

Questions owners ask before reaching out.

What do you actually implement?

Practical AI-assisted workflows using existing tools and systems where possible. Typical work includes lead follow-up, missed-call recovery, booking handoff, inbox triage, summaries, drafting, and process documentation.

Do you build custom software?

Not as the default offer. Operive is a productized implementation service built on existing AI tools and business systems. If a workflow clearly needs custom work later, that is a separate conversation, not the starting point.

Is this just AI training?

No. Training is part of implementation, but the offer is not generic education. The goal is to install one working process, train the team around it, and improve it over time.

How do you handle trust and safety?

We define where human review belongs, what kinds of data need extra care, and how to use AI responsibly inside real workflows. We are not a law firm, compliance certifier, or security auditor, and we do not pretend to be.

Who is the best fit?

Small businesses that already feel operational friction from slow follow-up, missed inquiries, manual admin, or inconsistent process execution. Home services is the best initial fit.

What is the best first step?

Start with a workflow audit. Pick one workflow, measure the result, and expand only after the first implementation proves useful.
