Process

How Operive works

The goal is not “more AI”. The goal is a cleaner operating result.

01. Audit the bottleneck

Map the workflow, find the friction, and decide what should change first.

02. Implement the sprint

Configure the workflow inside your current tools where possible and keep the scope tight.

03. Train the team

Rollout includes clear usage guidance, review expectations, and operator buy-in.

04. Improve what works

Tune prompts, routing, and rules from real usage instead of gut feel or vendor hype.
