## Qualification rule
Promote a lead for human review only if it has:
- DFW-local operation
- 10-500 employee estimate or local owner/operator path
- A visible intake/contact/booking/dispatch workflow
- A plausible automation angle tied to speed-to-lead, missed calls, intake, quote follow-up, scheduling, or customer-service load

No external outreach until CEO approval.
